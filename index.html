Fly into Puerto Princesa International Airport (PPS) and take a van directly to El Nido — the journey takes approximately 5–6 hours and costs around ₱250–350. Vans depart regularly from the terminal area. Once in El Nido town, Maya Hostel is a short tricycle ride away in Corong Corong.

Yes! Our staff can help you book island hopping tours directly from the hostel. El Nido is famous for its stunning lagoons and hidden beaches — Tours A, B, C, and D are all available. We'll help you find the best option based on your interests and budget.

Absolutely — the rooftop is one of the best features of Maya Hostel! Enjoy stunning panoramic views of El Nido and the surrounding mountains while watching the sunset. It's the perfect place to meet fellow travelers and share stories from the day's adventures.

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). If you arrive early, we'll do our best to accommodate you or safely store your luggage until your room is ready. Late check-out may be available upon request, subject to availability.

Yes! Maya Hostel is a safe and welcoming environment for solo travelers of all backgrounds. We offer a female-only dorm with secure access for extra peace of mind. Our staff are always available and the social atmosphere means you'll quickly feel part of a community.

Your stay includes air-conditioned accommodation, fast WiFi, personal lockers (bring your own lock or rent one), access to all common areas, rooftop access, and our friendly team's help with planning your El Nido adventures. We also organize regular social events to help you meet other travelers.
